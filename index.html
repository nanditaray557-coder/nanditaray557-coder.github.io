Understand Your Skin Type

Every skin is unique. Identify your type to build the perfect care routine.

Oily Skin

Prone to excess sebum production, enlarged pores, and occasional breakouts. Needs lightweight, oil-free hydration.

Dry Skin

Lacks moisture, often feels tight or flaky. Requires rich, nourishing ingredients like ceramides and hyaluronic acid.

Combination Skin

Oily T-zone (forehead, nose, chin) with normal or dry cheeks. Needs targeted care for different zones.

Sensitive Skin

Easily irritated, red, or reactive to harsh chemicals. Requires fragrance-free, soothing formulations.
